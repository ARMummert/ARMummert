## About Me

Hi, I'm Amy

I am a software engineer who wants to build resilient software systems that solve meaningful problems, improve 
people's lives, and drive innovation. I enjoy solving complex problems, exploring new ideas, and continuously learning 
new skills. I value creativity, collaboration, continuous learning, and the opportunity to make a positive impact on 
others.

I live in Longview, WA with my family, our German Shepherd, Great Pyrenees, and two cats. I graduated from Oregon State
University in 2007 with a BS in Exercise & Sports Science and again in 2024 with a BS in Computer Science. Outside of 
engineering, I enjoy reading, rock hounding, hiking, and occasionally video games.

I have a strong desire for learning and growth, and I am committed to continuous improvement in both my personal and 
professional life. Whether I'm developing software, exploring the Pacific Northwest, or learning a new skill, I am 
happiest when I am challenged and motivated.

I bring to software development strong problem-solving skills, adaptability, and the ability to remain calm under 
pressure while making thoughtful decisions.

Currently, I'm working with a senior software engineer, focusing on systems desing, reviewing project architectures, 
debugging applications, evaluating implementation decisions, and strengthening engineering skills.

My technical approach emphasizes Test Driven Development, Scalability, Clean Architecture, and CI/CD.
